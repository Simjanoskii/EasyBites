# Update the AI log

Use this workflow when the student asks to document AI use or completes work with AI assistance.

1. Read `docs/AI_LOG.md` and identify the section for the current milestone. Create the file or section if it is missing.
2. Ask the student which AI tool and model they used, what work it helped with, and the most important prompt(s). Use known conversation details, but do not guess a model or invent exact prompt wording.
3. Ask whether the AI was wrong or produced poor work. For each confirmed case, record what was wrong, how it was noticed, and how it was fixed.
4. Draft an entry that records the tool/model (or notes when the model is unknown), task, important prompt(s), generated work, and what the student kept, changed, or rejected.
5. Include at least two real mistakes/poor outputs per milestone when the student confirms them. Never fabricate examples to meet a count.
6. Show the proposed entry to the student and wait for confirmation before editing `docs/AI_LOG.md`.
7. After confirmation, update only the relevant milestone section and show the saved entry.
